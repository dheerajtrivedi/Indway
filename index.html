One factory, the full LT range.

Every lineup starts from your single-line diagram and load list. We size busbars, breakers and enclosures to your fault level, then build to IEC 61439 and IS 8623.

  • Power control centrePCC

    Main incoming and distribution from transformer and DG sources, with ACB incomers, bus couplers and interlocking.

    Up to 6300 A

  • Motor control centreMCC

    DOL, star-delta, soft starter and VFD feeders in fixed or drawout modules, with remote control and status wiring.

    Up to 4000 A

  • Power factor correctionAPFC

    Automatic capacitor banks with a relay controller, detuned reactors and contactor switching to cut reactive power charges.

    50 to 1000 kVAr

  • Distribution boardsMLDB, PDB, SDB

    Main and sub distribution for lighting and power circuits in buildings, hospitals and campuses.

    Up to 1600 A

  • AMF and synchronisingDG panels

    Automatic mains failure changeover and multi-DG synchronising with load sharing controllers.

    Up to 4000 A

  • Feeder pillarsOutdoor

    Weatherproof enclosures for street lighting, townships and site distribution.

    IP55 enclosure

From drawing to commissioning.

You get approval drawings before we cut steel, and an open invitation to witness the tests before we ship.

  1. Review

    We study your SLD, load list and site conditions, and flag anything that needs a second look.

  2. Engineering

    GA drawings, wiring diagrams and a bill of materials, sent to you for approval.

  3. Fabrication

    CRCA sheet steel is cut, bent, welded and powder-coated in-house.

  4. Assembly

    Busbars, switchgear and control wiring are fitted and ferruled to the approved drawings.

  5. Testing

    High-voltage, insulation resistance and functional tests, with your team welcome to witness.

  6. Commissioning

    Dispatch, installation support and on-site commissioning with a full test report.
