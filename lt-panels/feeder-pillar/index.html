Overview

Feeder pillars are built for the outdoors. The enclosure has a sloping canopy to shed rain, gasketed doors and a raised plinth, and is finished for UV and weather resistance. Inside, the layout is the same as a distribution board: an incomer, busbars and protected outgoings.

For street and area lighting we add automatic switching through an astronomical timer or photocell, with contactors per circuit and an energy meter, so lights come on at dusk and go off at dawn without anyone visiting the pillar.

Frequently asked questions

What is a feeder pillar used for?

A feeder pillar is an outdoor distribution panel. It takes power from the supply cable and distributes it to street lights, buildings or equipment on a site, with protection and switching for each outgoing circuit.

What does IP55 mean for a feeder pillar?

IP55 means the enclosure is protected against dust ingress that would harm the equipment and against water jets from any direction, which suits outdoor installation in Indian weather.

Can street lights switch on automatically?

Yes. We fit an astronomical timer, which calculates sunset and sunrise for your location, or a photocell, driving contactors for each lighting circuit.
