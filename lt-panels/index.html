Product range

LT panels, designed and built in New Delhi.

Every Indway lineup starts from your single-line diagram and load list. We size busbars, breakers and enclosures to your fault level, fabricate and wire in-house, and test before dispatch, building to IEC 61439 and IS 8623.

The range

  • Power control centrePCC

    The main LT switchboard of a plant or building.

    Up to 6300 A

  • Motor control centreMCC

    One lineup that starts, stops and protects the motors in your plant.

    Up to 4000 A

  • Automatic power factor correctionAPFC

    Keeps your power factor close to unity as the load changes.

    50 to 1000 kVAr

  • Distribution boardsMLDB, PDB, SDB

    The boards that take power from the PCC to each floor, wing or plant area.

    Up to 1600 A

  • AMF and synchronising panelsDG panels

    Starts your generators when the grid fails and hands the load back when it returns.

    Up to 4000 A

  • Feeder pillarsOutdoor

    Weatherproof distribution for outdoor sites.

    IP55 enclosure
